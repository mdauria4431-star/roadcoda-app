Pay & billing types

One list of the work that gets paid and/or billed. Tick Pay driver and/or Bill customer and set the defaults; a driver's own rate (on their record) beats the pay default. Each = per time it happens (fueling, strapping…); % of revenue is pay only. Only chosen customers = it pays and bills only on customers that turn it on (Customers page › Activities & driver pay), where you can also set that customer's price and driver pay. Miles, stops, days, hours and loads are billed from each customer's rate sheet or contract, so leave their Bill side off.
