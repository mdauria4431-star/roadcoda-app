Customer logins

Give a customer their own login to portal.html. A billing login sees its loads (and its DCs'), stop times and counts, PODs, its invoices and its activity file. A delivery login — a store or site — sees only the deliveries to its locations: times, counts, issues, PODs. No prices, no other customers. Customer logins can't open any office screen.
