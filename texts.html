Text messages

Texts go to stores for the same events as their notification emails (set the number and tick that they agreed on Customers › the location), and to drivers who switch texts on in the driver app. Nobody is texted without their agreement on file, and anyone can reply STOP. A driver's reply to a text lands in Messages; a store's reply shows below.
