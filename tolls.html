Tolls

Upload a toll statement

The Excel or CSV transaction list from E-ZPass, Bestpass, PrePass or any toll account. Each toll is matched to a load by the truck's toll tag or plate and the time it went through the plaza. Uploading the same file twice is safe: rows already on file are skipped.

Testing with the sample data? Download a practice statement built from this month's sample loads (made-up amounts, plus one unknown tag and one toll with no load).

Billing by customer

Matched tolls are the load's toll cost. The weekly invoice bills them when the customer's contract has a Tolls line. Tolls that arrive after a load was already invoiced show as arrived after invoice: add them to the customer's next draft invoice.
