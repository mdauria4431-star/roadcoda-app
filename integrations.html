Integrations

Connect your own PC*MILER and ELD accounts (paid add-ons). Each key is tested with the provider before it's saved, then stored encrypted. It is never shown again, not even to you; to change it, paste a new one. Only the owner can connect or disconnect.

PC*MILER options

Used for customers set to PC*MILER miles (Rates › customer › Miles billed on). Leave the truck profile blank to use PC*MILER's standard 5-axle, 80,000 lb truck.

Trucks and ELD vehicles

RoadCoda matches ELD vehicles to your trucks by VIN, then by unit number ("T-102" = "t102"). Fix any that are wrong or missing here. Locations update every 5 minutes.
