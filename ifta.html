IFTA fuel tax

Review before filing. This is a worksheet from your ELD mileage and fuel records. You file the return on your base jurisdiction's IFTA site, which works out the tax. Check the numbers against your records — RoadCoda doesn't check your base state's rules.

Fix before filing

By jurisdiction

Taxable gallons = taxable miles ÷ fleet MPG. Net = taxable − tax-paid gallons (negative = credit).

By truck

ELD miles against the truck's odometer history, and each truck's MPG.
