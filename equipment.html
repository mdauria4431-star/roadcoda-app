Truck device

A phone or tablet that stays in this truck (often the ELD device). Pair it once; at each shift the driver taps their name and enters the last 4 of their CDL, and sees only their loads on this truck.
    UnitPlateVINToll tag · classRegistrationInspectionPM dueDevice

    Pair the truck device

    On the device: open RoadCoda (from its home screen), tap Set up this device for a truck and type the code, or tap Scan sign-in code and scan this. Good for 15 minutes.
