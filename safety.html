Incidents

OSHA's injury logs are separate from DOT: they cover employees hurt at work — on the road, at a dock, in the yard — whose injury needed more than first aid, time off, light duty, or was a significant diagnosis. Add the people hurt on each incident (Incidents › the incident › People hurt). Keep one log per establishment — if everyone reports to one place, use Whole company. Logs are kept 5 years; post last year's 300A summary from February 1 to April 30; if OSHA requires your establishment to file electronically (Injury Tracking Application), it's due March 2. These printouts are equivalent forms (29 CFR 1904.29(a)); take close calls on what counts to your safety advisor.
