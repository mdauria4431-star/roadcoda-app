On time — the driver arrived by the close of the delivery window (stops with no window aren't counted). Late by — the average for the late ones only. Time on site — arrival to departure at the store. ETA kept — arrivals within 15 minutes of the planned time (arrivals more than 4 hours off, from a load re-planned after the fact, are left out). Issues — deliveries marked short, damaged, over, refused or closed. Claims — claims filed for this customer or its loads, by the date of the loss. Actual miles — from hub readings, when drivers enter them. Everything under this customer (its DCs and accounts) is included. The customer sees the same scorecard in their portal (Loads › Scorecard).
